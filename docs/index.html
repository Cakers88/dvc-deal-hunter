What is ROFR?

ROFR = Disney's "right of first refusal." Every resale contract goes to Disney after the seller accepts your offer. Disney has about 30 days to either waive (the sale goes through to you) or buy the contract itself at your price. If Disney takes it, your deposit comes back and you start over — no money lost, but you lose the deal and weeks of time.

Disney mostly does this to contracts priced below market, so a very cheap ask is more likely to get taken. The ROFR tag compares the asking $/pt with the highest prices Disney has actually bought back at that resort over the past 13 months (from DVC Resale Market's monthly ROFR reports): low = priced above any recent buyback, medium = inside or just above that range, high = at or below Disney's latest buyback, or a resort where Disney buys back 10%+ of contracts.
